Pink.Quanta is a _digital garden_ – a curated zettelkasten of collective knowledge.

The purpose of this site is to facilitate exchange between like-minded thinkers and divergent perspectives, fostering ==transdisciplinary== insights and [[Syntopy|syntopic concepts]], that integrate academia, science, society and art.

I’m convinced that [[Communication|communication creates a sur-plus]] and that we can achieve amazing things through sharing our knowledge, and having conversations about the things we are each passionate about.

> [!review] **This site is currently “aped” (AIP = Abandoned In Place).**
> 
> At 31 years old, I am slowly learning that real progress requires focus — committing to one direction and consciously saying no to everything else.
> 
> While I enjoyed being a *jack of all trades*[^1] and exploring many topics simultaneously for many years, it became increasingly overwhelming as I went deeper into each field.
> 
> I have made a commitment to manage my energy more deliberately and to realign my efforts with my goals.
> 
> As a result, working on this site is not a priority at the moment.
> My full focus for 2026 is reserved for our startup[^2].
> 
> Thank you for all the good conversations and interesting discussions. I look forward to reconnecting at another time and hope you are well in the meantime.
