*Systems thinking* is a cognitive framework that focuses on understanding the *interrelationships* among elements within a whole system.

Rather than isolating parts of a problem, systems thinking emphasises patterns, feedback loops, and the dynamic interactions that shape outcomes over time.
**It requires moving beyond linear cause-and-effect reasoning** and considering how changes in one part of a system affect the whole.

*Systems thinking* highlights the importance of context, structure, and long-term consequences.
